“The exquisite corpse will drink the new wine.” the game’s first sentence, Paris, 1925

Exquisite Corpse is a parlor game: each player writes a few words on a sheet, folds it to hide what came before, and passes it on. This table seats a language model. Every turn adds one to five words to the newest line, and only that line stays visible. Reveal the sheet whenever you like, and the whole poem unfolds with a close reading beside it.
